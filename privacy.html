Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use www.forex24hr.com (the forex market-hours clock) and app.forex24hr.com (TradeSagar, our live charting app), and by the forex24hr browser extension, why, and the choices you have. All three are run by forex24hr ("we", "us"). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Our sites and extension

SiteWhat it is
www.forex24hr.comFree forex and stock-market hours clock: which sessions are open, London–New York "golden hours", countdowns in your time zone. Includes a contact form.
forex24hr browser extensionShows which forex sessions are open and can alert you when a session opens or closes. See section 2(f) — it collects no personal data.

You can use the sites and the extension without creating an account.

2. Information we collect

a) Collected automatically on both sites

  • Technical data our servers and hosting / security providers log when you load a page: IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, date and time. Used to run the sites, keep them secure and fix problems.
  • Analytics: www.forex24hr.com uses Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to count visits and see which pages are used. Both sites use Hotjar to understand how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, anonymous session recordings and heatmaps) and to run optional short surveys. Hotjar masks text you type; we do not collect passwords or payment details through it.

b) Advertising

  • www.forex24hr.com shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • app.forex24hr.com may show ads from Adsterra. Adsterra may use cookies or similar technology to deliver and measure ads.

c) Stored only in your browser (TradeSagar)

Most TradeSagar settings never leave your device. They are kept in your browser's local storage and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data:

  • chart settings, chart tabs, drawings, indicators and their settings, watchlist, theme, panel positions;
  • your alerts and their log, including an email address or webhook URL if you enter one for alerts;
  • which indicators may send signals to your MT5 Receiver EA (sent only to 127.0.0.1, i.e. your own computer).

d) Sent to our server only when needed (TradeSagar)

  • Online counter: a random anonymous ID (not linked to you) is sent about once a minute while the page is open, to show how many people are online. It is kept in memory for two minutes and not stored.
  • Prediction record: when the price prediction is switched on, its call for each closed candle and any buy/sell signal (symbol, timeframe, prices and odds — nothing about you or your device) are sent so we can measure how accurate they really are. Kept up to 180 days.
  • Alert delivery: when an email or webhook alert fires, the alert text and the email address or webhook URL are sent to our server so it can deliver it. We keep no copy after delivery apart from normal server logs.
  • MetaTrader 5 accounts (optional, algo trading): if you connect an MT5 account, its login, password and server are stored on our server encrypted, only to place the trades you configure. You can remove an account at any time on the Accounts page, which deletes its saved login details from our server (a short activity log of its trades may remain).

e) Contact form (www.forex24hr.com)

If you contact us, we receive the name, email address and message you send, and use them only to reply.

f) The forex24hr browser extension

The extension does not collect personal information, your browsing activity or the content of websites you visit. It keeps only two things, locally in your browser's extension storage on your device:

  • whether session alerts are switched on or off;
  • the current market state (which sessions are open), used to show the badge and decide when to alert you.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the extension.

4. How we use information

  • to provide the sites and the features you choose (charts, alerts, automated trading you set up);
  • to keep the services secure and working, and to fix errors;
  • to understand how the sites are used and improve them;
  • to show advertising that keeps the free service running;
  • to answer your messages.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help run the sites (hosting and security, Google, Hotjar, Adsterra, email delivery), under their own privacy terms, or when the law requires it. Market data on the charts comes from third-party feeds; your personal data is not sent to them.

6. How long we keep it

  • Browser-stored settings: until you clear them.
  • Server logs: for a limited period needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Connected MT5 accounts: until you remove them.
  • Analytics and advertising data: as set by Google, Hotjar and Adsterra.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything TradeSagar stored on your device.
  • Opt out of personalised Google ads at Google Ads Settings; block cookies in your browser.
  • Remove a connected MT5 account on the Accounts page at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

Both sites are served over HTTPS. Trading-account passwords are stored encrypted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The sites are about financial markets and are not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version; important changes will be noted on the site.
