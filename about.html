About TradeSagar

TradeSagar is the free live charting app of forex24hr.com, the forex market-hours clock. It runs in the browser on desktop and phone — no download or sign-up needed to view charts.

What you can do

  • Live charts — forex pairs, gold, silver, crypto and indices, 1-minute to daily.
  • Chart types — candles, Heikin-Ashi, line, OHLC and Order Flow (footprint), the animated Ocean view (how to read it), plus the Pressure chart in Pro mode.
  • Chart tabs and layouts — several charts in tabs, or two and three charts side by side.
  • Indicators — RSI, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, Market Profile, supply/demand and smart-money zones, London Breakout and more.
  • Chart patterns — harmonics, head and shoulders, double tops and bottoms, triangles, wedges, flags and cup and handle.
  • Next-candle odds — statistical projections that show their own past accuracy.
  • Market context — economic calendar, trader positioning and sentiment beside the chart.
  • Multi-timeframe meter — buyer versus seller pressure across three timeframes at a glance.
  • Alerts — price and indicator alerts by pop-up and sound (email and webhook in Pro mode).
  • Drawing tools — trend lines, Fibonacci, positions, screenshots and shareable chart links.
