Ocean View: the market as a voyage

"Sagar" means ocean, and our logo is a boat. Ocean View (chart type Ocean (story)) draws the live chart of the symbol you pick as an animated sea, so you can see who is in control and where the danger lies without reading candles.

The whole idea in one picture

The boat is the price. When price goes up, the water and the boat rise; when it falls, they sink. Above the boat is a sellers wall (ice), below it a buyers floor (reef). Near the floor, look for buys. Near the wall, look for sells. In the middle, wait.

What everything means

You seeIt means
⛵ Boat + "PRICE NOW"The live price. The tag flashes green ▲ when price ticks up and red ▼ when it ticks down.
Water behind the boatWhere the price has been (the last 50 candles).
🧊 Ice wallSellers are waiting at this price. Price often stops or turns down here.
🪸 Reef floorBuyers are waiting at this price. Price often stops or bounces up here.
🎈 Green balloonsBuyers pulling the boat up. More and bigger balloons = stronger buyers.
⚓ Red anchorSellers dragging the boat down. When sellers are stronger the boat sinks lower and splashes; when buyers are stronger the balloons lift it.
🌊 Big wavesA busy market: lots of trading, fast moves.
🏁 Green flagTarget: where a trade takes its profit.
⛔ RocksExit if wrong: where a trade is closed to keep the loss small (stop loss).
💥 Ice or reef breakingPrice broke through the wall or floor. It can keep going that way.

The info box, top left

  1. Who is stronger: buyers, sellers, or even, with a buyers-versus-sellers bar.
  2. Big picture: is the price generally going up, down or sideways.
  3. Wall and floor: their prices and how far away they are.
  4. What to do: wait, watch for a buy or sell at the floor or wall, or follow the open trade to its target or rocks.

The story of a trade

  1. Approach. The boat sails toward the wall or the floor.
  2. Trade starts. If price reacts there, a BUY or SELL starts: a yellow line marks the start, a green flag the target, rocks the exit.
  3. Halfway. Half the profit is banked; the rest can no longer lose.
  4. Finish. Reaching the flag sets off fireworks. Hitting the rocks closes the trade with a small loss.

Where the data comes from

Prices are the live feed of the chart. Zones, signals, the tide and storm warnings come from the same Pressure analysis as the Pressure chart, run on closed candles only. Buyer and seller shares are estimated from each candle's tick volume.

Ocean View is a picture of the data for learning, not trading advice. Signals are rule-based and unproven — see the disclaimer.
